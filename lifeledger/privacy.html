LifeLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

LifeLedger ("the game") is a life and personal-finance simulation game for Android. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

In short: your game progress stays on your phone. LifeLedger has no account system of its own and no servers that receive your personal data. The game uses Google services for anonymous usage statistics, ads in the free version, and optional leaderboards. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

1. Information the game handles

InformationWhere it staysWhy
Game progress (the name you give your character, career, money, decisions, achievements, settings)Stored only on your phone.To save your game.
Everything in the simulation (income, debts, health, family, credit score and so on)Stored only on your phone. These are fictional game values, not your real finances or health.To run the game.

The game does not ask for and does not access your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, phone number, or real financial or health information.

2. Usage statistics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect usage statistics. These contain an app-instance identifier, basic device information (model, Android version, language, country) and the game events described above. They do not contain the name you give your character or the contents of your save.

Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can limit it by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads between decisions, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to undo a decision). AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where the law and your choices allow.

You can turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Players who remove ads or have Pro do not see full-screen ads. More about how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Purchases

Purchases such as LifeLedger Pro or removing ads are handled by Google Play. The game never sees your card or payment details. It only learns whether a purchase was made.

5. Leaderboards and achievements

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your scores (for example net worth at retirement in the game) and unlocked achievements to Google so they can appear on leaderboards. This is optional. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.

6. Sharing

When you choose "Share my life", the game creates an image of your in-game results and opens Android's share sheet. The image is saved to your phone's gallery so it can be shared. It goes only to the app or person you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

7. Your control and deletion

Your game data is on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. To limit advertising and analytics data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. For data held by Google Play Games, use the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

8. Children

LifeLedger is made for adults and older teenagers. It includes mature themes such as debt, relationships, loss and ethical dilemmas. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Not financial advice

LifeLedger is a game. Its tips, markets, loans and outcomes are simplified simulations for entertainment and education. Nothing in the game is financial, legal, medical or investment advice.

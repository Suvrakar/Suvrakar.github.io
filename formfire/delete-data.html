Delete your FormFire data

FormFire: AI Personal Trainer (Android) · Last updated: 27 October 2026

FormFire has no accounts. Your health answers, workouts, body data and settings are stored only on your phone, so you can delete them yourself at any time.

How to delete your data

  1. Delete everything in the app: open FormFire, go to Profile, scroll down and tap Reset all data, then confirm. This permanently erases all FormFire data on your phone straight away.
  2. Or uninstall FormFire. Android deletes all of the app's data from your phone.
  3. Usage statistics (only if you turned them on): email kar.suvra6682@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FormFire data". We will ask Google to delete the analytics data for your app install and reply within 30 days. You can also turn statistics off at any time in Profile.
  4. Ads (free version): reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and change your ad choices in Profile → Ad privacy choices where offered.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Health check answers, fitness profile, workout history, Body tab data (height, weight, calories, safety answers), settings, remindersDeleted immediately by Reset all data or uninstalling. Never stored anywhere else.
Usage statistics (only if turned on)Deleted on request, as above. Otherwise Google Analytics keeps them for at most 14 months.
Camera images and voiceNever stored or sent, so there is nothing to delete.

What is kept, and why

  • Purchases: Google Play keeps the record of a FormFire Pro purchase or subscription, as required for billing and refunds. FormFire does not store it. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Play Store app (Profile → Payments and subscriptions).
  • Health Connect: workouts FormFire saved to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect. Reset all data never deletes Health Connect data.
  • Ads: data Google collects to show ads follows Google's privacy policy.

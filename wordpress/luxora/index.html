What's included

AJAX cart & mini cart

Items go into the cart without a page reload. A slide-out mini cart updates live.

Wishlist

Shoppers can save products and come back to them on a dedicated wishlist page.

Quick view

A product's details and add-to-cart open in a modal, right from the grid.

Live product filters

Filter by category, price and more, with results that load as you go.

Order tracking

A built-in track-order flow so customers can check status without logging in.

Customizer colors

Change accent, primary and secondary colors from the WordPress Customizer.

SEO & schema

Product structured data and meta tags generated for richer search results.

My Account, redesigned

Custom WooCommerce dashboard, login and navigation, with profile avatar upload.

One-click demo import

Ready-made sections

Hero slider, deals of the day, tabbed products, promo banners, testimonials, brands and blog.

Extra pages

Templates for Deals, New Arrivals, Wishlist and Contact.

Docker ready

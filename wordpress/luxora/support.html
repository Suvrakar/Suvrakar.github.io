Luxora support

Questions, bug reports and setup help for the Luxora theme.

Contact

Email kar.suvra6682@gmail.com with "Luxora" in the subject. I usually reply within two working days.

Include in your message

  • Your WordPress, WooCommerce and PHP versions
  • The Luxora version (Appearance → Themes → Luxora)
  • What you expected and what happened, plus a screenshot if you can
  • Any plugins you've recently added or updated

Before you write

  • Make sure WooCommerce is installed and active. Luxora's shop features need it.
  • Try turning other plugins off one at a time to rule out a conflict.
  • If the layout looks broken after an update, clear any caching plugin and your browser cache.

Privacy

Luxora is a theme that runs on your own WordPress site. It doesn't send your store's data, or your customers' data, to me or to anyone else.
